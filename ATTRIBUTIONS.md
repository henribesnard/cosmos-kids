> ‘The best Milky Way map by Gaia’ — ESA/Gaia/DPAC, Stefan Payne-Wardenaar, licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO. The source file was renamed locally; its pixels are unchanged.

The Milky Way face-on reference comes from [ESA/Gaia](https://www.esa.int/ESA_Multimedia/Images/2025/01/The_best_Milky_Way_map_by_Gaia) under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 IGO](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/igo/). This image is an **artist’s impression based on Gaia data**, not a photograph of the Milky Way seen from outside. The interactive spiral-arm geometry is a separate reconstruction informed by Reid et al. (2019), [DOI 10.3847/1538-4357/ab4a11](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/ab4a11), and is not derived from the JPEG pixels.
