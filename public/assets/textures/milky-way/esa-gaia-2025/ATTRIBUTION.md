## Scientific status

This image is a face-on **artist’s impression based on Gaia data**, not a photograph of the Milky Way seen from outside. COSMOS KIDS must identify it as an illustration or reconstructed view.

The interactive spiral-arm geometry is a separate scientific reconstruction informed by Reid et al. (2019), [“Trigonometric Parallaxes of High-mass Star-forming Regions: Our View of the Milky Way”](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/ab4a11). It is not extracted from this JPEG and must not be described as an exact reproduction of the Gaia illustration.
